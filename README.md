# Listing Fixer

A free AI skill that fixes the selling side of a digital product listing. You already have the product. Listing Fixer helps with the rest.

## What it produces
- A ready-to-paste product description
- Launch price, later price, and when to raise it
- Package tiers (optional)
- A "what you need" requirements block
- A one-page licence template
- An optional upsell, downsell or cross-sell block with a link placeholder

## What it does not do
It does not name your product, design covers or research your market.

## Install
Add the `listing-fixer` folder (or the ZIP) as a skill in any AI tool that supports SKILL.md skills. If your tool does not support skills, paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into a chat as instructions, and share the `references/` files when asked.

## Use
Say "fix my listing" and answer the ten intake questions.

## Notes and limits
- Price ranges are example anchors for prompt and template products, supplied by the author. No market research was performed.
- The licence is a template, not legal advice.
- Not yet tested on any specific platform. Run `evals/test-prompts.md` to check behaviour in your tool.
