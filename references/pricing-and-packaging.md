# Pricing and packaging

Status of these numbers: **example anchors for prompt, template and small digital-tool products**, supplied by the skill's author. They are not market research. Adjust for other product types (courses, ebooks, software) and say so.

## Example tier anchors
| Tier | Typical range | Typical contents |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $29-49 | The core product, a short setup guide, 2-3 samples |
| Pro | $79-129 | Standard plus a video walkthrough, tested presets or variants, a template for adding the buyer's own details |
| Commercial / app licence | $199-499 | Rights to build the product into an app or to resell. Charge for this separately, because it carries most of the value |

## Launch vs later price
- Launch price: roughly $19-29 for early buyers, mainly to collect testimonials.
- Later price: the standard tier price once there are a few reviews.
- Say when to raise: after a set number of sales or reviews, or on a set date, and tell buyers in the listing if the price will rise.
- Never claim a price rise that is not planned.

## Package design
- Tiers should differ by real added value (video, presets, rights), not by removing basics.
- Name tiers plainly: Standard, Pro, Commercial.
- Three tiers at most.
- A single price is fine. Suggest a Pro tier later, not now.

## Requirements block format
**What you need:**
- [AI tool or software]: tested on [list only tools the seller says they tested]
- [File or format abilities required, e.g. can generate PDF or image files]
- [Skill level or time needed]

## Sanity checks
- Price far above similar products: ask what justifies it.
- Price very low for a commercial licence: warn it undervalues resale rights.
- Pay-what-you-want with a $0 minimum suits free lead magnets only.
