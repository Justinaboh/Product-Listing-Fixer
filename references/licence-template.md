# Licence template

This is a template, not legal advice. Tell the seller to have a lawyer review it before relying on it for high-value products.

## Base text (personal and client use, no resale)
**Licence for [PRODUCT NAME]**
Issued by [SELLER NAME] ("the Seller") to the purchaser ("you").

1. **What you may do.** Use [PRODUCT NAME] for yourself and for your clients' projects.
2. **What you may not do.** Resell, redistribute, share, repackage, rebrand or give away [PRODUCT NAME] or any part of it, whether free or paid. Do not publish it online.
3. **Ownership.** The Seller keeps all rights. You receive a licence to use it, not ownership.
4. **One licence, one buyer.** The licence covers the buyer named on the purchase. Teams need one licence per person unless the Seller says otherwise in writing.
5. **No guarantee.** [PRODUCT NAME] is provided as is. Results depend on how you use it.
6. **Refunds.** [REFUND POLICY or "see the product page"].
7. **Contact.** [EMAIL].

## Variants
- **Personal only:** delete "and for your clients' projects" in clause 1 and add "Do not use it for client work."
- **Commercial / app licence:** add "You may build [PRODUCT NAME] into your own product or service and sell that product. You may not sell or give away [PRODUCT NAME] itself as a standalone item or prompt."
- **Resale allowed:** replace clause 2 with the exact resale terms the seller states. Do not invent terms.

Always match the variant to the seller's answer to intake question 9.
